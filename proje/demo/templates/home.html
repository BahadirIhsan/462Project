Password + typing rhythm

A stolen password is not enough if it is not typed the way you type it. This localhost demo enrols your typing rhythm (key hold times and latencies, the same 3n+1 features as the CMU benchmark) and checks it at every login with the detectors evaluated in our report: Scaled Manhattan, a One-class SVM and, for the CMU password, the Siamese embedding.
